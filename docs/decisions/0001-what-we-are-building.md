# 0001 — What we are building

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-07

## Context

Aman & Manjeet are building a dairy (8–10 buffalo) whose core business is bulk
whole-milk sales to sweet shops. Separately, we want to run a retail
home-delivery business fed by that dairy.

## Decision

Build a **retail milk home-delivery venture** as a separate entity from the
dairy. Sell whole buffalo milk to households on **daily delivery with no
commitment required**, then convert casual buyers into regular fixed customers.

The dairy is our supplier, not our partner in this venture.

## Consequences

- We depend entirely on the dairy for supply — that relationship must be made
  formal (see 0002).
- "No commitment" is our main differentiator; the whole system must make casual
  ordering effortless while still nudging toward regular subscriptions.
- Success is measured first by **number of regular fixed customers**, not
  revenue or volume.
