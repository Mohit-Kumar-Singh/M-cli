# 0005 — Go-to-market

- **Status:** Accepted (with open items)
- **Date:** 2026-09-07

## Positioning

> **Pure, unadulterated buffalo milk. Delivered. Order as little as you want.
> No monthly lock-in.**

The contrast we're selling against:

- **vs. packet milk (Amul / Mother Dairy):** fresh, pure buffalo milk, not
  standardised/toned packet milk; delivered to the door.
- **vs. the local doodhwala:** no dilution, and no forced fixed quantity or
  month-long commitment — order day to day if you want.

## The "regular" offer

**What the customer commits to:**

- A fixed daily quantity, delivered every morning.
- They **pay even if they forget to pause.** Pausing must be done before the
  nightly order cutoff; miss it and that day's milk is delivered and billed.

**What they get for committing (the "priority" package):**

1. **Guaranteed supply on short days** — if milk is tight, regulars are filled
   first and casual orders are cut. Costs us nothing on a normal day; fits the
   buffer model.
2. **Price protection** — when we later raise the rate or add a delivery fee,
   regulars stay at ₹60/kg for **6 months** from signup.
3. **Earlier delivery slot** — regulars are first on the morning round.
4. **Priority for ad-hoc extras** — a regular asking for extra milk tomorrow is
   served before a new casual order.

Casual customers get none of these — same price today, but no guarantee, no
lock, later slot.

## Customer acquisition

- **Channel: word of mouth.** Start in our own locality, seed with people we
  know, and ask every casual customer for a referral.
- No paid marketing, no flyers at launch.

## First-15 plan (the expansion gate)

Target: **15 regular fixed customers** before scaling area/staff/price (see
0003). Approach:

- Launch to our own locality only.
- Convert people we already know first.
- After each casual customer has had a good week, pitch them the regular offer
  (price-lock is the hook).
- Ask satisfied customers to refer one neighbour.

## What we track from day one (all in the app — see 0006)

Per customer: name, address, phone, casual/regular, fixed qty, referral source.
Per day per customer: ordered qty, delivered y/n, delivered qty, paid y/n,
method (UPI/cash), amount.
Per day overall: milk taken from dairy, sold, returned to dairy.
